MAGRIFT — Privacy Policy

Developer: Paracord Studio · Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: MAGRIFT is a single-player action game for players aged 13 and over. We do not ask for your name, phone number, photos, contacts or location. The game creates a random player ID so your progress can be saved in the cloud, and lets you choose a nickname for the weekly league, leaderboards and the in-game chat. Optional rewarded ads are shown through Appodeal and its ad partners; in Europe we ask for your consent first. Game statistics and crash reports are sent only if you say "Yes", and you can turn them off at any time. For players who tell the game they are under 13, Appodeal, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are never started, no push-notification token is registered, and there is no chat. You can delete your account in the game's settings or on this website.

1. Who we are

MAGRIFT (the "Game") is developed by Paracord Studio ("we", "us"). We decide how the personal data described here is used. If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to paracordstudio7@gmail.com.

2. Age and children

  • The Game is not directed at children under 13. Its very first screen asks for your year of birth with no year pre-selected, before any SDK starts. Only the year is stored, and only on the device: it is not sent to us or saved in the cloud. It is asked once and cannot be changed in the settings.
  • If the year shows that you are under 13, the Game runs in a limited mode: the Appodeal advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are not started at all and no Firebase Cloud Messaging token is registered; ad buttons are hidden and every ad reward comes by waiting instead; there is no chat and no notifications, and other players see only an automatic name like "Mechanic-4F2A" in the league and the race; co-op is possible only with friends who enter a lobby code, with four quick signals and no text, and never with strangers. Cloud saving, purchases through the store and the network time check (section 12) still work. When the year of birth shows that the player has turned 13, the Game switches to the normal mode and shows the consent screens first.
  • We do not knowingly collect more data from children under 13 than this limited mode needs. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings on the device: currencies (bolts and cores), workshop, robot bodies and skins, unlocked zones, codex, achievements, tasks, language, sound and display settings, your year of birth and your player ID. A short log of recent runs is kept on the device and is attached to a support request only if you tick the box for it (section 8). To keep daily rewards and timers fair, the Game also keeps a time anchor: the last confirmed network time, how long the device had been running at that moment and the phone's restart counter. None of it leaves the device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Account and player ID

  • On first launch the Game signs in anonymously with Unity Authentication (Unity Gaming Services), which creates a random player ID. It is not linked to your name, e-mail address or phone number. A short 8-character code of it is shown in Settings for support requests.
  • If Google Play Games is signed in on your device, the Game links your player ID to that profile so your progress can be found again on a new device. This is optional. Google handles the sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy; we receive an identifier for the profile, not your e-mail address.

5. Cloud save

Your game progress (everything listed in section 3 except sound and display settings and your year of birth) is stored with Unity Cloud Save under your player ID, together with the time of the last save and a progress score, so you can continue on another device and recover progress after a reinstall. If two devices hold different saves, the Game asks which one to keep and keeps the other as a backup for 30 days.

6. Nickname, league, leaderboards, race, co-op and chat

  • Nickname. By default you are "Mechanic-XXXX". You may choose your own nickname (3–16 characters). It is checked on our server against a word filter and is shown to other players in the league, the leaderboards, the race, co-op and the chat.
  • League and leaderboards. When a run ends, the Game sends its result to our server (Unity Cloud Code and Leaderboards): zone, time, whether the boss was defeated, number of evolutions, level, kills and the run's start and end time. The server uses this to award league points, place you in weekly groups of 30 players and the all-time zone records, and to detect impossible results (cheating). Other players see your nickname, league tier, robot body, best times and score, never your player ID. Weekly groups are formed by the power of your workshop, which the server reads from your cloud save.
  • Race and challenges. In the race you play the week's track against recordings of other players' runs. When a race ends, the Game sends the result and a compact recording of the run (score every 2 seconds, robot position four times a second and key events, up to 4 KB) to our server (Unity Cloud Code), which checks it and updates your race rating. Checked recordings are shown to other players as see-through rivals with your nickname, robot body, paint and score. Rivals are picked by rating and by workshop power, read from the cloud save. A challenge code (6 characters, valid for 7 days) lets a friend race against your recording; both of you see the result.
  • Co-op ("Together"). When this mode becomes available, you can play a run with friends who enter your lobby code. The lobby and the connection go through Unity Lobby and Unity Relay, which handle your IP address for the session. Players in the lobby see each other's nickname, robot and four quick signals ("Over here!", "Help!", "Thanks!", "Hooray!"); co-op has no text chat.
  • Chat. Players aged 13 and over can chat in rooms by game language using Unity Vivox text channels. Messages, emoji and shared run cards are visible to everyone in the room; each room shows its last 50 messages. Links and phone numbers are removed and a word filter is applied. You can hide the chat in Settings.
  • Reports and blocking. If you report a message, the report (message text, sender, room, time and reason) is stored for moderation for 90 days. Your list of blocked players is stored in your cloud save. Players who break the Terms of Use can be hidden from the chat or the league.

7. Notifications

  • The Game can remind you about ready rewards, ending tasks and seasons with notifications scheduled on the device (no server involved). It asks for the phone's notification permission (Android or iOS) only after you agree in the Game.
  • For news about the Game (for example, a new zone) and league notices, the Game registers a push token with Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). The token is stored with your player ID and language and is used only to send these notifications. On iPhone and iPad, Firebase delivers them through Apple Push Notification service.
  • You can turn notifications off in the Game's settings (all or by category) or in the phone's settings. For players under 13 no notifications are scheduled and no push token is registered.

8. Support requests

If you write to us from Settings → Support, we receive your topic and message, your player ID, the Game version, the device model, the operating system version and the language. If you tick the box, the log of your last run is attached. An e-mail address is optional and is used only to reply to you. Requests are stored with Unity Cloud Save and kept for 12 months after we close them. If you write to our e-mail address directly, we keep the correspondence for the same time.

9. Game statistics and crash reports (only with your consent)

  • On first launch the Game asks: "Help us make the game better?" Only if you answer Yes does it send anonymous gameplay statistics to Google Firebase Analytics and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. Before that, collection is switched off. For players under 13 neither service is started and the question is not asked.
  • Statistics are linked to a random Firebase installation ID, not to your nickname, player ID or contacts. They include events such as session length, tutorial steps, run start and end (zone, robot body, result, time, level), upgrade and evolution choices, currency spent and earned, shop offers shown and bought, ads offered and watched, tasks, achievements and setting changes, plus device model, operating system, language, country (from the IP address) and app version. Crash reports contain the error, device state and app version.
  • Turn it off at any time: Settings → Game statistics. Collection stops and the data stored on the device for Firebase is reset. Firebase keeps statistics for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

10. Advertising

  • The Game shows rewarded video ads only, and only when you tap a button marked "Ad" (for example, to continue a run once, double the bolts of a run, or collect a daily parcel). There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. Watching is always optional: every ad reward can also be obtained by waiting, and an ad only skips the wait. With the "No Ads" purchase, rewards are given without ads. For players under 13 the advertising SDK is never started.
  • Ads are delivered through the Appodeal mediation platform, which runs an auction among its ad partners (section 13). The partner that wins shows the ad.
  • Consent in Europe. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent form (IAB Transparency & Consent Framework v2.2) before any ad is requested. The form lists the ad technology partners that may process your data: the ad partners in section 13, the companies they work with to resell ad space, and Google's list of commonly used ad technology providers (see also the IAB Global Vendor List). If you do not consent, ads are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time: Settings → Privacy.
  • United States. Residents of US states with privacy laws can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the same Settings → Privacy option.
  • Advertising ID. Appodeal and its ad partners may use your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iPhone and iPad, Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking). You can reset or delete it in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

11. Purchases

In-app purchases are optional and are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store) through Unity In-App Purchasing. We never see your card or payment details. We receive the purchase receipt (product, order ID, time, purchase token) to deliver the item and to restore "No Ads" and other one-time purchases after a reinstall; a copy of what you bought is kept in your cloud save.

When we add or remove a partner, we update this list and the effective date.

14. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the Game you asked for (performance of a contract): account, cloud save, purchases, league, leaderboards, chat and support.
  • With your consent: personalized ads, game statistics and crash reports, push notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings; this does not affect what happened before.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the Game secure and fair (anti-cheat, word filter, moderation of reports), non-personalized ads that fund a free game, and answering support requests.
  • Legal obligations, for example keeping purchase records.

15. International transfers

Our partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, they rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

17. Your rights and choices

  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or the CCPA/CPRA and other US state laws), you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw consent. Write to paracordstudio7@gmail.com with your player ID (Settings → Player ID). We answer within 30 days.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account, or follow the steps on this page if the Game is no longer installed.
  • In the Game: Settings → Privacy (ad consent), Game statistics, Notifications, Hide chat.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Sharing of advertising data with ad partners for targeted ads can be turned off as described in section 10.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

18. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with the providers listed above, who protect it with technical and organizational measures. Access to the moderation and support data is limited to the developer.

19. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the Game or its partners change. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date; important changes are also mentioned in the Game or in the store's "What's new" notes.
