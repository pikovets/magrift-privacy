MAGRIFT — Delete your account and data

Developer: Paracord Studio · Updated: 16 November 2026

You can delete your MAGRIFT account and the data linked to it at any time, for free: in the game, or by e-mail if the game is no longer installed.

Option 1. In the game

  1. Open MAGRIFT and tap the settings button (gear) at the top of the main screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and hold the button for 3 seconds.

The account is deleted right away; the game then starts again from the beginning.

Option 2. Without the game (by e-mail)

  1. Send an e-mail to paracordstudio7@gmail.com with the subject MAGRIFT account deletion.
  2. Include your player ID (8 characters, shown in Settings → Player ID). If you no longer have it, give your nickname and the approximate date you last played, and send the e-mail from the address you used if you ever wrote to our support.

We confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days. We may ask one question to make sure the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • Your player account in Unity Authentication and its link to Google Play Games.
  • Your cloud save: progress, currencies, items, achievements, and the backup copy, if any.
  • Your nickname, league standing and leaderboard records.
  • Your push-notification token and your support requests.
  • Game statistics and crash reports linked to your installation: the game resets them on the device, and we send a deletion request to Google Firebase.
  • The game's data on your device (when you delete in the game).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Chat messages you already sent stay in the room's last 50 messages, shown as from a "Deleted player", until newer messages replace them.
  • Reports other players made about your messages are kept for up to 90 days for moderation.
  • Purchases made with real money are recorded by Google Play or the App Store under their own policies. You can restore one-time purchases such as "No Ads" in a new game with "Restore purchases".
  • Data received by ad networks is kept as described in their policies (see the Privacy Policy, section 13); you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Delete only some data

You can also ask us to delete only certain data without deleting the account — for example your nickname, your support requests or your league records — by writing to the same address. To stop game statistics, turn off Settings → Game statistics; to remove your push token, turn off notifications.
